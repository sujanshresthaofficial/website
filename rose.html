A Letter to you

Dear,

        Warm congratulations! for stepping into next year of your life. Another year has come around, and somehow you manage to make every single one better than the last. Today isn't just a date on the calendar — it's a celebration of everything you are: the warmth you bring into a room, the way you laugh with your whole heart, and the quiet, steady kindness you give without ever asking for anything back.

I hope today feels exactly the way you deserve — slow when you want it slow, sweet the whole way through, and full of the little things that make you smile. And I hope you know, even on the ordinary days, how loved you are.

Here's to you, Rosy. Here's to another year of us.

Yours, always
